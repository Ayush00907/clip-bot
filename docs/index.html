clip-bot

clip-bot is a personal automation tool used by the owner of the ClipVerse YouTube channel. It edits short vertical clips from videos the owner is permitted to use (their own footage, Creative Commons videos, or creators who gave permission) and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
